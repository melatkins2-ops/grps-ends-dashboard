About the ENDS

The ENDS define the scholar outcomes GRPS monitors over time.

Graduation & Advancement

Graduation, ninth-grade on-track credits, SAT performance, advanced coursework, and postsecondary enrollment.

Academic Achievement

Academic growth, state assessment proficiency, college-readiness benchmarks, and English learner growth.

Personal Responsibility & Belonging

Attendance, suspension, and scholar sense of belonging.

How measures are displayed

Each ENDS domain opens with three headline measures selected to represent the outcome at a glance. Remaining adopted measures are preserved under “Additional Measures” and remain fully accessible.

School ENDS applicability

Each school now has a grade-span-based applicability matrix. Measures that do not apply to a building are excluded from its At a Glance view rather than appearing as missing. Applicable measures without validated building data are explicitly labeled “Data not yet loaded.”

All-school attendance coverage

ENDS 3.1 Chronic Absence is now populated from the GRPS 24–25 Attendance Comparison source across the active school directory wherever a matching building record exists. The dashboard includes 2023–24 and 2024–25 school rates and 2023–24 subgroup rates. Subgroup cells with n<10 are suppressed.

Building-level ENDS coverage

The Schools page now separates data that map directly to an ENDS measure from supporting/context information. Current building-level sources include school CNA workbooks, M-STEP school reports, M-STEP target-analysis files, and district school-profile data. Missing results are left missing rather than inferred.

Building-level validation rules

Direct source means the value is stated in the school CNA. Calculated means it is transparently computed from source-stated components. Derived means it is mathematically inferred and is not treated as an official KPI. Approximate preserves the source wording. Context only is not promoted to an ENDS measure when the reporting year, numerator, denominator, or exact definition is incomplete.

School reporting

The Schools page is a viewing tool only. It shows preloaded school profile data and validated school-level evidence when available. Data-loading controls are intentionally excluded from the public-facing dashboard.

Subgroup reporting

Validated subgroup data are now available for four-year graduation, SAT composite benchmark, and NWEA MAP growth. Small-n suppressed results are not displayed. Grade-specific M-STEP subgroup values remain in source documentation but are not combined into district grades 3–7 ENDS rates without denominators.

Data & methodology

Definitions, sources, suppression rules, release status, and technical data controls are available for staff who need them.
